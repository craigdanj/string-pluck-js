Guitar Pluck JS
GitHub ↗Web Audio · no dependencies

Make a string sing.

A small plucked-string synthesizer for the browser. Tap a string, shape the response, and find your sound.

Standard tuning · E A D G B E

Tap or click a string. Keyboard: 1–6 for individual strings, Space to strum.

Sound controls

Attack controls the initial fade. Decay controls sustain. Damping softens repeated string vibrations; pick position changes the harmonic shape.

Runs locally in modern browsers. Sound starts on your first interaction.
